Match Settings

Match Attitudes

  • PIC = Play It Cool (conserve energy)
  • NORMAL = Normal match settings
  • MOTS = Match of the Season

Effects on Team Spirit (TS)

League / Cup / Relegation

  • PIC: TS increases by +1/3.
  • MOTS: TS is halved.
  • In export mode, these factors can be adjusted.

Friendly / Challenger Cup / Consolation Cup

  • PIC and MOTS have no effect on TS.
  • In Hattrick, friendly matches are always played as NORMAL.

Hattrick Masters

  • PIC and MOTS have a smaller effect on TS than usual.
  • Team settings:
    • Playing Match of the Season (MOTS) or Play It Cool (PIC) results in a smaller decrease or increase in Team Spirit than in normal matches.
    • TS decreases by approximately 1/3 or increases by approximately 1/4.
    • However, the effect during the match is the same as in a normal match.

Important Information About Cup Matches

  • Effects on TS, confidence and fan mood only apply to:
    • National Cup
    • League Cup
  • Other cup competitions count as friendly matches.
  • Therefore, always select the match type Friendly for Challenger Cup and Consolation Cup matches.

National Teams

  • For national teams (NT) and U21 national teams (U21 NT), Team Spirit decays more slowly than usual.
  • The calculation logic for national teams is activated through the resting value when it is set to a value greater than 4.50.
  • The starting TS for national teams is 5.0, just like the resting value.
  • National teams do not have a coach in the same way club teams do.
  • Nevertheless, the coach Leadership (FQ) value can be used in the calculator to calibrate TS decay.
  • Based on current observations, an FQ value of 8 provides a good approximation of national team TS development.
  • The match type Tournament is intended for typical national team tournaments.
  • The match types League, Cup and Relegation are also supported.
  • The tournament schedule generally needs to be created manually. An example that can be imported is available in the download area.
  • It is useful to export an existing schedule and then import it again for further editing.
  • The first event in the default schedule is preset to Reset. Depending on the scenario, this entry can be modified or removed.
  • The new Match Type update can also be used to overwrite incorrect entries when necessary. This works like an empty entry.
  • The midfield rating in home matches behaves in the same way as in away derbies for club teams.

Team Spirit (TS)

Team Spirit (TS) directly influences midfield strength and is therefore one of the most important factors determining a team's overall performance.

At the beginning of a season, the default resting value is composed (TS 4.5). With a Sports Psychologist, this resting value can be permanently increased to a maximum of TS 5.0.

TS affects the playmaking performance of all players contributing to the midfield rating. The higher the Team Spirit, the stronger the resulting midfield rating.

General Guidelines

  • Each TS level above "composed" adds approximately +7% to midfield ratings.
  • Each TS level below "composed" causes a penalty of slightly more than -7%.

Factors Affecting Team Spirit

Coach Leadership

A coach with high Leadership helps stabilize Team Spirit and slows down its decay.

Match Attitude

The selected match attitude has a significant impact:

  • Play It Cool (PIC) increases TS by approximately 33%.
  • Match of the Season (MOTS) decreases TS by approximately 50%.

Transfers

Player transfers can negatively affect Team Spirit. A Team Spirit drop following a transfer can be simulated using a Drop entry.

Sports Psychologists

Sports Psychologists help maintain Team Spirit at a higher level.

Note:

The actual TS value achievable before league and cup matches may vary slightly depending on the country, update times and player transfers.

Example: Calculating Hidden Team Spirit (TS)

In the lineup simulator, only integer TS values are available for selection.

Internally, however, Hattrick works with more precise intermediate values. These can be approximated by observing changes in the midfield rating (MF).

Example

  • TS "Content" = 6.5 → MF = 10.50
  • TS "Satisfied" = 7.5 → MF = 11.25

A difference of 1.0 TS point therefore results in a change of 0.75 MF points.

If the Sandbox mode displays an MF value of 10.75 for the current skill level, the calculation is as follows:

1. Calculate the Difference

10.75 - 10.50 = 0.25

2. Calculate the TS Fraction Using Proportional Scaling

TS fraction = 0.25 / 0.75 = 0.333

3. Add the Fraction to the Starting TS

6.5 + 0.333 = 6.83

Result

TS ≈ 6.83

This calculation is based on linear interpolation between two known TS/MF values.

Training Intensity Adjustment

One short-term way to increase Team Spirit is to reduce Training Intensity (TI).

The general rule is:

  • Lower Training Intensity → Higher Team Spirit
  • Higher Training Intensity → Lower Team Spirit

This method is often used before particularly important matches, such as relegation matches or decisive cup games.

Formula

TS = TS * pow(newTI / oldTI, -0.5115)

Important Disadvantages

  • Loss of training effectiveness.
  • Risk of a drop in player form.
  • Negative effects on long-term background form.
The Training Intensity adjustment should therefore only be used in exceptional circumstances.

Timing of the Adjustment

For this measure to take effect, Training Intensity must be reduced before the last daily update preceding the training update.

To minimize training losses, it is recommended to increase Training Intensity again immediately after the match.

Example: Graphical Comparison of Multiple TS Plans

Exported CSV files can also be analyzed graphically using external programs.

One example is Gnuplot, which can be used to compare several TS plans with each other.

The following example uses three exported TS plans and displays their midfield development across all events in a single graph.

In addition, the midfield development for PIN, PIC and MOTS is displayed for the plan ts-export-3.csv.

Run the Gnuplot Script

gnuplot ts-export.gp

Contents of ts-export.gp

set terminal wxt

set datafile separator ";"

set title "MF across Events"
set xlabel "Event"
set ylabel "Value"
set grid
set autoscale

plot 'ts-export-1.csv' using 2:11 with linespoints title 'MF PIN PIC PIC', \
     'ts-export-2.csv' using 2:11 with linespoints title 'MF PIN PIN PIC', \
     'ts-export-3.csv' using 2:11 with linespoints title 'MF PIC PIC PIC', \
     'ts-export-3.csv' using 2:17 with linespoints title 'MF Away PIN', \
     'ts-export-3.csv' using 2:18 with linespoints title 'MF Away PIC', \
     'ts-export-3.csv' using 2:19 with linespoints title 'MF Away MOTS'

pause -1

The labels PIN PIN PIC, PIN PIC PIC and PIC PIC PIC identify the TS strategies used.

The CSV files must be located in the same directory as the Gnuplot file unless different paths are specified in the script.

Midfield Rating Reference

Midfield Away PIN serves as the reference for calculating midfield ratings.

Based on this reference value, the different combinations of venue and match attitude (PIC / PIN / MOTS) are calculated.

The midfield rating for a match is always calculated using the current Team Spirit (TS).

This makes it possible to see which midfield rating can be expected under the respective conditions.

Available Variants

  • Midfield Home PIN
  • Midfield Home PIC
  • Midfield Home MOTS
  • Midfield Away Derby PIN
  • Midfield Away Derby PIC
  • Midfield Away Derby MOTS
  • Midfield Away PIN
  • Midfield Away PIC
  • Midfield Away MOTS

The column Midfield in Match subsequently displays the value applicable to the match based on the currently selected venue and match attitude.

The currently selected combination is additionally highlighted using colors.

This makes it easy to identify which calculated variant is being used for the current midfield rating.

After a reset, row 1 should display the configured MF reference value in the Midfield Away PIN column.

Choosing the Reference Value

The reference value can be selected so that the team achieves this value when playing Away + PIN with a TS of 4.5.

The remaining values can then be interpreted as projections based on this reference value.

Example:

If the MF reference value is set to 10, Away + PIN at TS 4.5 will correspond to a value of 10.

The other fields show how the midfield rating changes depending on Team Spirit (TS), venue and selected match attitude.

The TS value of 4.5 represents the resting value to which Team Spirit is reset at the beginning of a new season by the seasonal reset.

Determining the Reference Value from a Known Match

The reference value does not necessarily have to come from a match played with Away + PIN at TS 4.5.

An already completed reference match can also be used, provided that the following information is known:

  • Team Spirit (TS)
  • Venue
  • Match attitude (PIN / PIC / MOTS)
  • Actual midfield rating achieved

The reference value is then adjusted so that the corresponding combination of TS, venue and match attitude in the table matches the known midfield rating of the reference match.

The remaining columns can subsequently be used to determine the midfield ratings that would result from other combinations of TS, venue and match attitude.

The reference value therefore remains based on the defined standard of Away + PIN at TS 4.5.

However, the reference match itself does not need to meet these conditions.

Example

A team achieved a midfield rating of 11.00 in a known match with a specific TS, venue and match attitude.

The reference value is adjusted until the corresponding column in the table displays 11.00 for exactly these conditions.

The same table can then be used to determine which midfield rating would have been expected under alternative conditions.

This means that any known match can be used as a reference, provided that TS, venue, match attitude and midfield rating are known.

Using this method, midfield ratings can essentially be converted to alternative conditions for a match.
